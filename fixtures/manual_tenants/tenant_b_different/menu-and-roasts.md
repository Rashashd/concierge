# Tenant B: Coffee Roastery Menu

Tenant B is Northstar Roasters, a neighborhood coffee roastery and tasting bar.

## Espresso Bar

Northstar Roasters serves espresso, cortado, cappuccino, flat white, batch brew, iced coffee, and seasonal cold brew. The house espresso is a medium roast blend with notes of cocoa, almond, and orange peel.

## Retail Beans

Retail bags are sold in 250 gram and 1 kilogram sizes. Current single-origin options include Ethiopia Guji, Colombia Huila, and Guatemala Antigua.

## Food

The tasting bar offers butter croissants, zaatar buns, banana bread, and a rotating vegan cookie. Food items are prepared each morning and may sell out by afternoon.

## Brewing Advice

For pour-over, Northstar recommends 18 grams of coffee, 300 grams of water, and a total brew time near 3 minutes.

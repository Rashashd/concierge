# Tenant B: Classes and Events

Tenant B is Northstar Roasters, a coffee roastery that hosts public classes.

## Brewing Classes

Intro to Pour-Over is held every Saturday at 10:00 AM. The class covers grind size, water temperature, pouring technique, and recipe adjustment.

## Cupping Sessions

Public cupping sessions are held on the first Thursday of each month at 6:30 PM. Guests taste three coffees and learn how to compare aroma, acidity, body, and finish.

## Private Events

Private tastings are available for groups of 6 to 14 people. Requests should be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

## Age Guidance

Guests under 16 may attend tasting events with an adult, but espresso machine workshops are limited to guests 18 and older.

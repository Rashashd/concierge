# Tenant B: Locations and Subscriptions

Tenant B is Northstar Roasters, a local coffee roastery with one public tasting bar.

## Location

The tasting bar is located at 42 Cedar Lane, Portland. It is open Tuesday through Sunday from 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM.

## Subscriptions

Coffee subscriptions ship every 2 weeks. Subscribers can choose whole bean or ground coffee and may select light, medium, or dark roast preferences.

## Pickup

Local pickup orders are ready after 11:00 AM on the next business day. Pickup orders are held for 5 days.

## Equipment

Northstar sells filters, hand grinders, kettles, and ceramic drippers. Espresso machines are not sold in the tasting bar.

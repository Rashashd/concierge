# Tenant A: Refunds and Cancellations

Tenant A is AcmeCare Policies, a service business that manages appointment-based consultations.

## Refund Policy

Clients may request a refund within 7 calendar days of payment if the consultation has not started. Refunds are returned to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.

No refund is available after a completed consultation unless AcmeCare Policies cancels the appointment or cannot provide the scheduled service.

## Cancellation Window

Appointments may be cancelled without a fee up to 24 hours before the scheduled start time. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment are charged a late cancellation fee of 35 USD.

## Rescheduling

Clients may reschedule once without a fee when the request is made at least 12 hours before the appointment. Same-day rescheduling is handled by support and is not guaranteed.

## Exceptions

Emergency cancellations are reviewed by the support team. Documentation may be requested before a fee waiver is approved.

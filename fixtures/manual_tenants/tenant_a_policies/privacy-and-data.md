# Tenant A: Privacy and Data Handling

Tenant A is AcmeCare Policies, a service business that stores appointment and billing information for clients.

## Personal Data

AcmeCare Policies collects client names, email addresses, phone numbers, appointment notes, payment status, and support history. Sensitive notes are visible only to authorized staff.

## Retention

Appointment records are retained for 3 years after the last client interaction. Billing receipts are retained for 7 years to satisfy accounting requirements.

## Data Export

Clients can request a copy of their records by contacting privacy@acmecare.example. Export requests are usually completed within 15 business days.

## Deletion Requests

Deletion requests are accepted when no legal retention requirement applies. If billing records must be retained, AcmeCare Policies deletes optional profile details while preserving the required receipt record.

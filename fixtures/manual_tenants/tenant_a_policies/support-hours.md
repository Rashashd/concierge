# Tenant A: Support Hours and Contact Rules

Tenant A is AcmeCare Policies, a service business with a weekday support team.

## Support Hours

Support is available Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern Time. Weekend support is limited to urgent appointment issues only.

## Contact Channels

Clients should use support@acmecare.example for billing, cancellation, refund, and account questions. Phone support is available for active appointment problems at +1-555-0100.

## Response Targets

General support requests receive a first response within 1 business day. Refund and data export requests may take up to 3 business days for the first response because they require account review.

## Escalation

If a client reports a safety concern, payment dispute, or repeated failed appointment connection, the conversation should be escalated to a human support specialist.

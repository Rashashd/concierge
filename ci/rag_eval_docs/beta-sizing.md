# Beta Outfitters Sizing Support

Outerwear fit questions are one of the most common pre-purchase support requests, especially during cold-weather launches. The chat team keeps a reference sheet for body measurements and preferred layering style.

Beta Outfitters offers jacket sizing help in chat, and shoppers should share height, weight, chest measurement, and preferred fit. Those details help the team explain whether a trim, regular, or roomier cut is the better match.

If a customer plans to wear insulated layers underneath, support agents usually mention that before recommending a final size. Exchange instructions are also included when the selected fit is between two sizes.

Boot and backpack sizing use separate charts, so the jacket workflow is kept distinct from footwear and gear support.

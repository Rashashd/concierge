# Beta Outfitters Returns Policy

Customers frequently ask whether they should keep the original box, whether shoes can be tried on indoors, and how long refunds take after the parcel arrives back at the warehouse. The support team answers these questions in a standard post-purchase guide.

Beta Outfitters accepts unworn items with tags for 30 days after delivery. Final sale accessories stay excluded from returns, even if the packaging is unopened.

The team recommends photographing any transit damage before opening a damage claim. Refund timing varies by payment method, but the warehouse still confirms item condition before the refund is released.

Gift returns can be converted into store credit when the original purchaser does not want money returned to the same card.

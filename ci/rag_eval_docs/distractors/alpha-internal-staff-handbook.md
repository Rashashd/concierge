# Alpha Clinic Internal Staff Handbook

This handbook is for Alpha Clinic staff only. It covers internal procedures that patients should not see. Several sections use vocabulary that overlaps with public-facing documents.

The staff entrance is on the west side of Cedar Avenue, near the loading dock. Employees should use badge access and sign in at the security desk before proceeding to the staff elevator. This is different from the patient entrance described in public arrival notes.

Shift scheduling for clinical staff follows a rotation model. Weekday coverage is planned to ensure at least two nurses and one physician assistant are on the floor during peak hours from 10 AM to 2 PM.

The internal cancellation log tracks same-day no-shows and late cancellations for quality review. Staff note that patients who miss appointments without notice are contacted within 2 hours to reschedule. This internal process does not change the public 24-hour cancellation policy.

Telehealth equipment setup is covered in the IT onboarding checklist. Clinicians should test camera and microphone connections before their first virtual session of the day. The telehealth platform used for staff training is the same one offered to patients for follow-up visits.

Insurance verification for staff health benefits is handled by HR separately from patient billing workflows.

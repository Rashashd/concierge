# Gamma Software Partner Integration Guide

This guide is for technology partners building integrations with the Gamma Software platform. It describes technical requirements that are not relevant to end customers.

Partner onboarding takes 15 business days and includes API key provisioning, webhook endpoint registration, and integration testing in the partner sandbox environment. This is separate from the 10 business day customer onboarding process.

Partners have access to a dedicated technical support channel from 9 AM to 5 PM Eastern on weekdays. This is different from the customer-facing priority support hours of 7 AM to 7 PM Eastern on weekdays.

Data export APIs for partners support CSV, JSONL, and Parquet formats, with an additional GraphQL endpoint for real-time data access. The GraphQL endpoint is not available to standard customers and is reserved for certified technology partners.

Audit log access for partners is limited to events related to their integration, with a retention window of 90 days. This is shorter than the customer-facing audit log retention of 365 days on business plans.

CRM integration partners can build custom connectors that extend the native HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive integrations. Partner connectors are reviewed by the Gamma Software integration team before being listed in the partner marketplace.

# Gamma Software Legacy Platform Documentation

This documentation describes a prior version of the Gamma Software platform. It is kept for reference only and does not reflect current product capabilities.

The legacy platform required a 30 business day onboarding process that included manual data migration, custom SSO setup, and on-site training sessions. This is significantly longer than the current 10 business day onboarding for standard teams.

Legacy support was available from 6 AM to 10 PM Eastern, seven days a week, with a dedicated phone line for priority accounts. The current platform provides priority support from 7 AM to 7 PM Eastern on weekdays, with overnight emergency monitoring.

Data exports on the legacy platform were available in XLSX and XML formats, delivered via nightly FTP to a managed server. The current platform supports CSV, JSONL, and Parquet exports to S3-compatible storage with configurable scheduling.

Audit log retention on the legacy platform was 180 days for all plans, with no enterprise extension option. The current platform retains audit logs for 365 days on business plans and offers 7 year retention for enterprise tenants.

Legacy CRM integrations included Zoho CRM and Microsoft Dynamics through custom webhook templates. The current platform offers native CRM integrations for HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive, with custom webhooks available on growth plans.

# Beta Outfitters Vendor Return Agreement

This agreement describes the return terms between Beta Outfitters and its product vendors. It is a business-to-business document and should not be confused with customer-facing return policies.

Vendors supplying Beta Outfitters with standard apparel items agree to accept unsold inventory returns within 60 days of delivery. This vendor return window is separate from the customer 30-day return policy and applies to wholesale transactions only.

Backpack vendors provide a 3 year manufacturer warranty for defects in materials and workmanship. Beta Outfitters passes through a 2 year warranty to customers, retaining the third year for internal quality review and vendor claims.

Shipping timelines for vendor replenishment orders are 10 business days from purchase order to warehouse receipt. This is the inbound supply chain timeline and is not related to the customer-facing 2 business day shipping promise.

The agreement includes provisions for store pickup coordination when vendors deliver directly to the Riverfront location for customer collection. This vendor-to-store delivery model is used for oversized items that cannot be shipped through standard carrier channels.

Sizing specifications from vendors are reviewed by the merchandising team before products are listed. Vendor size charts are compared against Beta Outfitters standards to ensure consistency across the product catalog.

# Alpha Clinic Follow-up Options

Patients often ask whether routine reviews must happen in person or whether a virtual slot is enough. The nursing team usually confirms the answer after checking whether new testing or a physical exam is needed.

Alpha Clinic offers telehealth follow-up visits for medication reviews, lab result discussions, and routine care planning. Video follow-ups are not used for procedures, imaging, or visits that require hands-on evaluation.

Portal messages can be used to confirm the camera link, browser support, and pharmacy details before the appointment starts. Patients who miss the video room can still receive a call from the care team if the clinician approves the fallback.

Interpreter coordination is available for virtual visits, although the clinic asks for extra lead time when a less common language is needed.

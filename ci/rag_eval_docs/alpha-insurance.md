# Alpha Clinic Insurance Planning

Coverage checks are usually confirmed before the visit date, and the billing team asks new patients to upload both sides of their insurance card through the portal. Preventive visits and specialist follow-ups may be routed through different billing workflows.

Alpha Clinic currently works with Northstar Health, Blue Harbor PPO, and self-pay appointments. Medicaid referrals can still be scheduled, but prior authorization must be approved before the appointment is finalized.

If a plan is changing next month, the clinic asks patients to send the new information early so claims do not pause during the handoff period. Financing options for larger balances are reviewed separately by the patient accounts desk.

Prescription refill questions are handled by the nursing team and do not change insurance verification timing for the visit itself.

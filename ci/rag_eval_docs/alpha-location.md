# Alpha Clinic Arrival Notes

Visitors coming from the highway usually enter from Cedar Avenue, while rideshare drop-offs are directed to the marked curb lane beside the pharmacy entrance. Parking validation is available for standard outpatient appointments.

Alpha Clinic is at 42 Cedar Avenue, Suite 300, and the easiest landmark is the north entrance parking garage. Patients who use elevator access should exit on the third floor and follow the blue wall signs.

Families bringing strollers are encouraged to use the wider hallway route near the imaging suite. The reception desk can also mail printed directions when a patient is referred from another office.

For evening pickups, security recommends meeting on the lit side of the garage because the service lane closes during loading windows.

# Beta Outfitters Pickup Instructions

Customers using buy-online-pick-up-in-store usually receive a second confirmation after the item is scanned into the front counter inventory. Busy weekends can change when the final ready-for-pickup email lands.

Beta Outfitters supports store pickup at the Riverfront location, and most pickup orders are ready within 4 hours. A photo ID and the order confirmation email are both recommended when the item is collected.

Large seasonal items may be staged in the stockroom instead of the front desk, so the collection window sometimes includes a few extra minutes for staff retrieval. The store can also authorize pickup by another person if support updates the order first.

Clearance goods that sell out locally may still be shipped even when store pickup was selected at checkout.

# Beta Outfitters Pack Warranty

Warranty coverage is discussed most often for commuter packs, hiking packs, and school backpacks that see daily use. Cosmetic wear is treated differently from functional failures during review.

Beta Outfitters backpacks include a 2 year warranty for normal-use defects, including zipper failures, seam defects, and broken buckles. Damage from cutting tools, fire, or clear misuse is reviewed outside the standard warranty path.

Support may request photos of the damaged area and the interior product tag before approving a repair or replacement. In some cases the brand ships replacement hardware instead of the full product.

Rain cover accessories and removable pouches can carry separate accessory terms depending on the manufacturer.

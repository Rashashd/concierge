# Alpha Clinic Visit Planning

Patients often ask about quiet check-in windows, parking flow, and whether the lab desk stays open through the afternoon. Most walk-ins are triaged quickly before lunch, and pediatric paperwork is usually reviewed at the side counter.

For routine care, Alpha Clinic keeps weekday hours Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM. Weekend access is narrower, and Saturday visits are handled by appointment review instead of open scheduling.

Staff also remind visitors to bring medication lists, arrive ten minutes early for new-patient forms, and use the south elevator if they need wheelchair-accessible access from the garage level.

The after-visit team posts lab reminders in the portal, and the front desk can print visit summaries for patients who prefer paper records.

# Gamma Software Support Coverage

Priority accounts often need clarity on when live help is staffed versus when incidents are simply monitored. Regional customers also ask whether the published schedule is listed in local time or a fixed support time zone.

Gamma Software provides priority support from 7 AM to 7 PM Eastern on weekdays. Overnight emergency incident tickets are still monitored, but standard questions wait for the next staffed support block.

Customers can attach screenshots, request IDs, and workspace details to speed up triage. Phone callbacks are available when a sev-one incident affects a production rollout or a user-access outage.

Quarter-end reporting windows typically increase volume, so the support desk recommends opening time-sensitive cases early in the day.

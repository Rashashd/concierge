# Beta Outfitters Shipping Promise

Order routing depends on warehouse load, item availability, and whether a package contains oversized camping gear. Most apparel and accessories leave from the regional fulfillment floor instead of the flagship store.

Beta Outfitters sends standard orders out within 2 business days. Express purchases placed before noon move faster and usually leave the warehouse the same day if stock has already been reserved.

Holiday promotions can add a brief packing delay, especially when gift wrapping is selected. Tracking emails are normally issued once the shipping label is scanned by the carrier.

International parcels follow a different customs workflow and are not part of the standard domestic promise listed above.

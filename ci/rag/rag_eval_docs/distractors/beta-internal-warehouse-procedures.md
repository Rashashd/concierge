# Beta Outfitters Internal Warehouse Procedures

This document describes internal warehouse procedures for Beta Outfitters staff. It is not customer-facing and should not be used to answer customer inquiries.

Standard orders are processed on the main fulfillment floor, with a target pick-and-pack time of 4 hours from order receipt. Orders that miss the morning cutoff are processed the following business day. This internal target is not the same as the customer-facing shipping promise.

Returns are inspected at the quality check station before being restocked or routed to the outlet. The inspection checklist includes verifying tags, checking for wear, and confirming item condition matches the return reason. This process supports the 30-day return policy but is not visible to customers.

Backpack warranty claims are reviewed by the product team, who assess whether the defect falls under normal-use coverage. Approved claims are either repaired in-house or replaced from reserve stock. The internal review timeline is 3 business days, which is not communicated to customers.

Store pickup orders are staged in the front counter holding area and scanned into the inventory system when ready. The internal target is to have pickup orders ready within 2 hours, but the customer-facing promise is 4 hours to account for variability.

Jacket sizing support uses a reference chart maintained by the merchandising team. The chart is updated quarterly and includes measurements for all current outerwear styles. This chart is used by the chat support team but is not published publicly.

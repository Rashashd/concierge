# Beta Outfitters Draft Holiday Shipping Guide

This draft guide is under review for the upcoming holiday season. It describes proposed shipping timelines that differ from the current standard policy and has not been published.

For the holiday season, Beta Outfitters is considering a standard shipping timeline of 5 business days due to increased warehouse volume. Express orders placed before 10 AM may still ship same day, but this is not guaranteed during peak periods.

The draft proposes an extended return window of 45 days for holiday purchases, compared to the standard 30-day window. This extension would apply only to items purchased between November 15 and December 31.

Holiday warranty claims for backpacks would be processed within 5 business days instead of the standard 10 business day turnaround. This is a temporary proposal and has not been approved.

Store pickup during the holiday season would be available at both the Riverfront and Harbor Kiosk locations, with a readiness window of 8 hours instead of the standard 4 hours.

The draft also mentions a proposed jacket sizing hotline for holiday shoppers, which would provide dedicated support for outerwear fit questions during peak shopping periods.

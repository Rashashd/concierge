# Alpha Clinic Holiday Schedule Archive

This archive records special holiday schedules from prior years. These schedules are not active and should not be used for current appointment planning.

During the winter holiday season, Alpha Clinic operated on a modified schedule of Monday through Wednesday, 9 AM to 3 PM. This reduced schedule was in effect only for the two weeks surrounding the holiday and is no longer applicable.

The holiday archive also notes that the clinic accepted walk-in flu vaccinations on select Saturdays from 10 AM to 2 PM during the fall campaign. This temporary service is not part of the regular weekday schedule.

Holiday cancellation policies were relaxed during the winter season, allowing same-day changes without a missed-visit charge. This exception was specific to the holiday period and does not reflect the standard cancellation policy.

Telehealth follow-ups were offered on an extended basis during the holiday season to reduce in-person traffic. This temporary expansion is not the same as the regular telehealth follow-up service.

Insurance acceptance did not change during the holiday period, but the billing office operated on reduced hours for payment plan consultations.

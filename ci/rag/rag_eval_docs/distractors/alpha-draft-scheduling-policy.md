# Alpha Clinic Draft Scheduling Policy

This document is a draft scheduling policy for internal review. It has not been approved for public distribution and contains provisional values that differ from current patient-facing guidance.

For routine care, Alpha Clinic is drafting a weekday schedule of Monday through Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM, with a reduced Saturday morning block from 8 AM to 12 PM. This draft is under review by the operations committee and may change before publication.

The draft also proposes a new patient portal feature for self-scheduling, which would allow patients to select available slots without calling the front desk. This feature is not yet live.

Staff parking and badge replacement procedures are covered in a separate internal handbook. The draft scheduling policy does not change those operational workflows.

A provisional cancellation notice period of 48 hours is being considered for specialist follow-up appointments. This is longer than the current 24-hour standard and remains under discussion with the clinical leadership team.

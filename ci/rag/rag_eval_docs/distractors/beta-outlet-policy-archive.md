# Beta Outfitters Outlet Store Policy Archive

This archive records policies that applied to the Beta Outfitters outlet store, which closed in 2024. These policies are no longer active but contain vocabulary similar to current customer-facing documents.

The outlet store offered standard shipping within 7 business days for online orders. This was longer than the main store's 2 business day promise due to the outlet's smaller fulfillment team.

Outlet returns were accepted within 14 days of delivery for unworn items with tags. Final sale items were excluded from returns, consistent with the main store policy but with a shorter return window.

Backpacks purchased at the outlet carried a 1 year warranty for covered defects, half the duration of the main store's 2 year warranty. The outlet warranty excluded cosmetic wear and normal-use damage from heavy outdoor activities.

The outlet store did not offer store pickup, as it operated as an online-only clearance channel. Customers who wanted to try items in person were directed to the Riverfront flagship location.

Jacket sizing support was available at the outlet via a posted size chart in the product listing. Live chat sizing assistance was not offered for outlet items, as the outlet team did not have access to the main store's measurement reference tools.

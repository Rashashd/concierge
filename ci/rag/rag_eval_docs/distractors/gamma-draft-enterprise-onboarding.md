# Gamma Software Draft Enterprise Onboarding Plan

This draft plan describes a proposed enterprise onboarding process that is under review. It has not been approved and differs from the current standard onboarding timeline.

For enterprise teams, the draft proposes a 20 business day onboarding plan that includes two admin workshops, a data migration rehearsal, and a production cutover weekend. This is longer than the current 10 business day standard team onboarding.

The draft includes a dedicated security review phase of 5 business days, during which the enterprise security team validates SSO configuration, access controls, and audit log settings. This security review is not part of the standard onboarding checklist.

Enterprise support coverage in the draft plan includes 24/7 phone support for the first 30 days after go-live, followed by the standard priority support hours of 7 AM to 7 PM Eastern on weekdays. This enhanced coverage is a proposed addition and is not currently offered.

The draft also proposes native CRM integrations for Microsoft Dynamics and Zoho CRM in addition to the current HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive connectors. These additional integrations are under development and are not yet available.

Data export formats in the draft plan include XLSX and XML in addition to the current CSV, JSONL, and Parquet options. The additional formats are being evaluated for enterprise customers with legacy reporting requirements.

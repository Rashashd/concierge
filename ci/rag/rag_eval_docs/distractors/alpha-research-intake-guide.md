# Alpha Clinic Research Study Intake Guide

This guide describes the intake process for patients enrolled in Alpha Clinic research studies. It is not a public-facing document and should not be used for general patient inquiries.

Research study participants are scheduled for intake sessions on Tuesday and Thursday from 10 AM to 4 PM. These sessions are separate from regular clinic hours and are conducted in the research wing on the fourth floor.

The research intake form asks participants about their medical history, current medications, and consent for data collection. This is different from the standard new patient paperwork used for routine visits.

Research study cancellations require 72 hours notice to allow the study coordinator to reassign the slot. This is longer than the standard 24-hour cancellation notice for regular appointments.

Telehealth follow-ups for research participants include additional data collection steps, such as symptom surveys and medication adherence questionnaires. These are not part of the standard telehealth follow-up service offered to general patients.

The research program accepts a broader set of insurance plans for study-related visits, including plans that are not accepted for routine care. This does not change the insurance plans accepted for standard Alpha Clinic appointments.

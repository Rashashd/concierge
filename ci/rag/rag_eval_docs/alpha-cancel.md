# Alpha Clinic Cancellation Procedures

The scheduling staff uses text reminders, phone reminders, and portal messages to reduce missed visits. Patients who know they may need to move a follow-up can usually request alternate slots online.

To avoid a missed-visit charge, Alpha Clinic asks patients to cancel or reschedule with at least 24 hours of notice. Same-day changes are reviewed case by case when the clinic can fill the open time.

Weather delays, school closures, and transportation issues should still be reported to the front desk so the chart reflects why the visit changed. Staff try to move urgent patients into the nearest open clinical block.

New patient paperwork remains valid for a short period, so rescheduling soon after a cancellation usually does not require repeating every intake form.
